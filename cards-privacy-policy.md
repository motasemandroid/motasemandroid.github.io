# Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 13, 2026

## Card Game Table

Card Game Table is a casual card game app. This privacy policy explains how the app handles information.

## Information We Collect

Card Game Table does not ask you to create an account and does not request personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or address.

The app stores basic game progress and preferences locally on your device, such as game settings, help dialog choices, and round counters. This information stays on your device and is not sent to us.

## Ads

This version of the app includes test ad placeholders for banner ads and video ad behavior. These test ads are used to verify the app experience during development and testing.

If production ads are added in the future, third-party ad providers may collect or process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, approximate location, app interactions, diagnostics, and ad performance data according to their own privacy policies.

## Children

Card Game Table is a general casual card game. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information because the app does not directly collect personal information.

## Data Security

Game settings and progress are stored locally on your device. No method of electronic storage is completely secure, but the app is designed to avoid collecting personal information directly.

## Changes To This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the effective date above.
