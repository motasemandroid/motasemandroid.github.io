Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to Pipes Game (package name: dana.farah.pipesgame), published by Dana Farah. This policy explains what information the game collects, uses, stores, and shares.

Information We Collect

Pipes Game does not require users to create an account and does not ask users to provide personal information such as name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, microphone access, or camera access.

The game stores gameplay progress locally on the user's device, such as the current level, tile positions, move count, completed levels, and related game state. This information is used only to allow the player to continue playing from where they left off. It is not sent to the developer's servers.

Advertising

Pipes Game may display banner ads, rewarded video ads, or interstitial video ads. Ads may be shown when a player chooses certain actions such as showing the full image, shuffling the puzzle, skipping a level, or after completing multiple levels.

If third-party advertising services are added to the game, those services may collect or use information such as advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location inferred from IP address, app interactions, ad views, and ad performance data to provide, measure, and improve advertising. These third-party services are responsible for their own data handling practices.

How We Use Information

Information used by the game is used to:

  • save and restore gameplay progress;
  • provide game features such as levels, puzzle state, preview, shuffle, skip, and completion;
  • display and manage ads if advertising services are enabled;
  • maintain and improve the game experience.

Data Sharing

Pipes Game does not sell personal information. Local gameplay progress is not shared by the game with the developer's servers. If third-party advertising services are enabled, those services may receive data as described in the Advertising section for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and related advertising purposes.

Data Retention and Deletion

Gameplay progress is stored locally on the user's device and remains there until the user clears the app data or uninstalls the app. Users can delete local game data through Android system settings by clearing the app's storage/data or uninstalling the app.

Children's Privacy

Pipes Game is a puzzle game and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If advertising services are enabled and the game is directed to children or families, only advertising services and SDKs appropriate for that audience should be used.

Security

The game is designed to keep gameplay progress locally on the device. If future features send information over the internet, reasonable security measures such as encrypted connections will be used where appropriate.

Third-Party Services

The game may use third-party services for advertising or app distribution. Users should review the privacy policies of any third-party services used in the app, including Google Play services and any advertising provider that may be integrated.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted by updating this page with a new effective date.
