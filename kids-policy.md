# Privacy Policy for Kids Games

Effective date: June 2, 2026

Kids Games is an educational game app for children. It includes memory games, picture puzzles, shapes, colors, numbers, letters, words, animals, sounds, mazes, sorting, sequences, and similar learning activities.

## Information We Collect

Kids Games does not ask users to create an account and does not directly collect personal information such as name, address, phone number, email address, or precise location.

The app may save simple game progress on the device, such as selected language, difficulty level, completed levels, hints, and coins. This information is stored locally on the device and is used only to improve gameplay.

## Ads

Kids Games uses Google Mobile Ads to display ads and rewarded ads. Ads are configured for child-directed treatment and limited to family-safe content settings.

Google and its advertising partners may collect or use limited device and advertising data to provide, measure, and improve ads, depending on the device settings and applicable laws. For more information, please review Google’s privacy information:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Children’s Privacy

Kids Games is designed for children and families. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that personal information has been collected from a child, please contact us so we can review and remove it if needed.

## Sharing

Kids Games does not sell personal information. The app does not include account features, chat, or direct user-to-user communication.

## Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services, including:

- Google Play services
- Google Mobile Ads

These services may process data according to their own privacy policies.

## Security

We aim to keep the app simple and safe. Because the app does not directly collect personal information, the main stored information is local gameplay progress on the device.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted in the updated version of this policy.
